# Keyboard side

*Config → Keyboard.*

Pick whether the keyboard plays **1P** or **2P**. It becomes a player of its own, with the keys you
already configured. Built for local play at a tournament: two players, one machine, one of them on a
keyboard.

The game gives the keyboard and the first controller the same player number, so in local versus they
drive the same character. Picking a side here moves the **controller** to the other one. The keyboard
does not move and its keys do not change.

It never writes your key settings, and it never moves the keyboard between the game's two keyboard
players. Both were tried and both broke something. If you have a second keyboard player set up in
the game's own options, those keys answer on the controller's side. Set *Keyboard Player Number* to
1 there to turn them off.

*Hold the side during a match* keeps writing both controller slots while a local match runs, so the
side you picked is the side you get. Turn it off to let the game decide.

Nothing here runs online.
