# Palettes

Put any colour on either character, live.

A colour belongs to the character, not to a side of the screen, so it stays with them when they cross
over. The mod remembers what each character wears and puts it back next time.

Palettes are normal `.pal` files, the game's own format (the one Hantei-kun writes). Put them in
`UNI2-IM\Palettes\<character>` with a name, an author and a description. Effect colours are part of
the palette and go with it. **Default** in the list puts the game's colours back.

Online, your palette is sent to the other player over Steam, outside the game. **See the other
player's colours** controls their side. On: you see what they picked. Off: their packets are dropped
and their side looks the way the game gives it. Yours is sent either way. A player without the mod
sees nothing.

**Palette Nativa** is something else: the game's own colour customiser, controlled from the overlay.
It builds a colour from the character's stock palettes, one per part, so you cannot pick any colour
you want. But the game saves it and **every** opponent sees it, with or without the mod.
