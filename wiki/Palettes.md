# Palettes

Any colour on either character, applied live.

A colour belongs to the character, not to a half of the screen, so it follows them across a
crossover. What each character wears is remembered and put back on next time.

Palettes are ordinary `.pal` files, the game's own format, the one Hantei-kun writes. They go in
`UNI2-IM\Palettes\<character>` with a name, an author and a description. Effect colours are part of
a palette and travel with it. **Default** in the list puts the game's colours back.

Online, your palette goes to the other player beside the game, over Steam. **See the other player's
colours** decides their side: on, you see what they picked; off, their packets are dropped and their
side looks the way the game gives it. Yours is sent either way. A player without the mod sees
nothing.

**Palette Nativa** is a different thing: the game's own colour customiser, driven from the overlay.
It builds a colour from the character's stock palettes, one per part, so you cannot pick any colour
you like. But the game saves it and **every** opponent sees it, mod or no mod.
