# Pause and frame stepping

**F5** pauses. **F6** steps one frame, hold to repeat.

Two freeze modes. *Tick stop* suppresses the whole game frame and is the default. *Hitstun Stop*
reuses the engine's own hitstop, which keeps menus alive but draws effects wrong. That comes with
the technique; it is not a bug.

Auto pause can stop the game on an attack, on an armoured move, or at a given hit of a combo, and
resume after a countdown.
