# Pause and frame stepping

**F5** pauses. **F6** steps one frame. Hold it to repeat.

There are two freeze modes. *Tick stop* skips the whole game frame and is the default. *Hitstun Stop*
uses the engine's own hitstop, which keeps menus working but draws effects wrong. That is how this
method works, not a bug.

Auto pause can stop the game on an attack, on an armoured move, or on a chosen hit of a combo, then
resume after a countdown.
